# AVALON COMPANION
## Complete Product Requirements, UI/UX Design System & Development Master Plan

You are a senior Flutter engineer, software architect, game systems designer, UI/UX designer, and QA engineer.

Your mission is to design and build a complete Android application called **Avalon Companion**.

Do not treat this as a simple prototype. Build it as a polished, reliable, offline-first mobile application that acts as an automated Game Master (MC) for physical games of The Resistance: Avalon.

The application must preserve the traditional physical-card gameplay while automating narration, game tracking, voting records, quest resolution, and victory conditions.

The final deliverable must be a working Android APK.

---

# PART 1 — PRODUCT VISION

## 1.1 Product identity

Product name: Avalon Companion

Product description:

An automated Game Master for physical Avalon games. One person operates the application while everyone else plays using physical Avalon cards.

The application handles the responsibilities normally performed by a human narrator.

Core philosophy:

- Physical cards remain central to gameplay.
- One Android phone controls the entire match.
- No internet connection required.
- No player-to-role mapping.
- No automated role distribution.
- No automatic identification of Merlin.
- The MC manually handles character distribution and assassination.
- The application handles all public game-state calculations.
- Narration supports English and Filipino.

## 1.2 Target platform

Primary: Android APK.

Framework: Flutter.

Programming language: Dart.

The architecture should allow future iOS support without requiring a complete rewrite.

## 1.3 Supported game size

5–10 players.

## 1.4 Character scope

Base Avalon only.

Supported characters:

GOOD:
- Merlin
- Percival
- Loyal Servant of Arthur

EVIL:
- Assassin
- Morgana
- Mordred
- Oberon
- Minion of Mordred

No expansions or custom characters in the initial release.

# PART 2 — CORE GAMEPLAY ARCHITECTURE

## 2.1 Physical gameplay model

Players use physical:
- Character cards
- Success cards
- Fail cards
- Approve voting cards
- Reject voting cards

The application is the MC controller.

The MC manually:
- Distributes character cards.
- Records public player names.
- Selects quest members.
- Counts physical votes.
- Counts physical Success and Fail cards.
- Handles Merlin assassination.

The app must never store a mapping between player names and secret roles.

## 2.2 Information privacy

There are two interface contexts:

PUBLIC GAME INFORMATION:
- Player names
- Current quest
- Required team size
- Current leader
- Public team proposals
- Voting results
- Quest results
- Public game status

PRIVATE MC CONTROLS:
- Settings
- Game configuration
- Narration controls
- Manual corrections
- Game management

Do not create a secret-role database.

Do not expose private information through notifications, logs, analytics, or screenshots.

Add an optional MC PIN to protect settings and sensitive administrative actions.

Gold is the primary accent; light gold highlights it and muted gold supports it without competing.

Cinematic artwork, layered gradients and soft gold glows are allowed — and expected — on backgrounds and hero surfaces, but they must stay behind content, never reduce text contrast, and must respect the reduced-motion setting. Decorative clutter on controls is still out.

`QuestProgressIndicator`, `NarrationPlayer`, `GameStatusIndicator`,
`OrnamentalDivider`); `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` holds the mapping. Do not create a

## 3.4a Motion

Motion is restrained and cinematic: fades, gentle scale, slow atmospheric
background drift and smooth phase transitions. Durations come from the `Motion`
tokens in `lib/core/theme/app_metrics.dart`; nothing hand-rolls a duration.

A pitch-black `#000000` background must never appear — the darkest surface is
`#0B0E14`. The app must honour the system's "remove animations" accessibility
setting: when reduced motion is on, drift, scale and looping ambience stop, and
transitions collapse to short fades.

## 3.5 Mobile layout

Design for Android phones first.

Requirements:

- Safe area support.
- Scrollable content when necessary.
- Large touch targets.
- One-handed usability.
- Readable text in dim environments.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Support different Android screen sizes.

The Game Master dashboard should prioritize important controls and minimize unnecessary navigation.

# PART 5 — OFFICIAL BASE GAME RULE ENGINE

Implement the game rules in a separate, independently testable Dart module.

Never put core rules directly inside Flutter widgets.

## 5.1 Team composition

| Players | Good | Evil |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5 | 3 | 2 |
| 6 | 4 | 2 |
| 7 | 4 | 3 |
| 8 | 5 | 3 |
| 9 | 6 | 3 |
| 10 | 6 | 4 |

## 5.2 Quest team sizes

| Players | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Q5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| 7 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| 8 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| 9 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| 10 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |

## 5.3 Quest failure rules

Normal quests:
At least one Fail card causes failure.

Quest 4:
For seven or more players, at least two Fail cards are required.

All other quests:
At least one Fail card causes failure.

## 5.4 Voting rules

Each player casts one physical vote.

Approval requires more than half of all votes to be Approve.

A tie is rejected.

Track consecutive rejected proposals.

Five consecutive rejected teams cause Evil victory.

A successfully approved team resets the rejection counter.

## 5.5 Leader rotation

The leadership token moves to the next player in seating order after each proposal resolution.

Implement this consistently for accepted and rejected teams.

## 5.6 Victory conditions

GOOD:
- Complete three successful quests.
- Survive Merlin assassination.

EVIL:
- Complete three failed quests.
- Cause five consecutive rejected teams.
- Correctly identify Merlin during assassination.

## 5.7 Game state machine

Implement explicit states:

- Setup
- Preparation
- Night
- DayDiscussion
- TeamSelection
- TeamVoting
- QuestResolution
- Assassination
- GoodVictory
- EvilVictory
- Paused

Only permit valid transitions.

Prevent:
- Skipping required phases.
- Resolving an incomplete quest.
- Starting a new quest after victory.
- Recording invalid vote totals.
- Recording invalid quest card totals.
- Resolving the same quest twice.

# PART 6 — NARRATION ENGINE

Build a reusable narration engine.

## 6.1 Script architecture

Narration must be stored separately from widgets.

Use structured narration data.

Each instruction should contain:
- Instruction ID.
- Phase.
- English text.
- Filipino text.
- Applicable character requirements.
- Sequence number.
- Optional pause duration.

## 6.2 Night narration behavior

The base-game narration must correctly support:

1. Everyone closes their eyes.
2. Evil players open their eyes and identify one another.
3. Evil players close their eyes.
4. Merlin opens their eyes and identifies Evil players except Mordred.
5. Merlin closes their eyes.
6. Percival opens their eyes and sees Merlin and Morgana.
7. Percival closes their eyes.
8. Everyone opens their eyes.

This is an initial narration sequence, not a rigid script for every possible role combination.

The system must conditionally include or skip instructions based on selected characters.

IMPORTANT:
Oberon is not known to the other Evil players.

Ensure narration does not accidentally instruct Oberon to identify teammates.

## 6.3 Voice system

Use Android Text-to-Speech.

Features:
- English narration.
- Filipino narration.
- Adjustable speech speed.
- Voice selection where available.
- Pause and resume.
- Replay.
- Skip.
- Volume control.
- Optional ambient sound.

Handle audio interruptions and unavailable language voices gracefully.
